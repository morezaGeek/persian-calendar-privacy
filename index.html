Privacy Policy

Persian & Gregorian Calendar (package: com.mrreza.persiancalendar) is provided by MRE Developer.

Last updated: October 6, 2026. This policy describes the Google Play edition and its current features.

Privacy contact: mo.reza2011@gmail.com.

1. Calendar and personal information

The app does not require an app account. Notes, event titles and descriptions, reminder dates and times, habit names and completion history are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Settings, your selected city and coordinates, weather cache, and an optional WeatherAPI key are also stored on the device. The app does not send these notes or habit records to a developer-operated server.

Some content can be displayed in widgets, notifications and reminder screens, including on the lock screen depending on your Android settings. You can control these features in the app and in Android's notification and lock-screen settings.

2. Location, city search and weather services

You can choose a city manually without granting device location permission. If you use Current Location and grant permission, the app accesses approximate or precise location according to the permission you grant, to select weather coordinates and support location-based features. Selected coordinates are saved for later weather updates. The app does not request background location permission; scheduled weather updates can use the coordinates already saved.

Weather requests send the selected latitude and longitude to the weather provider. Open-Meteo is the default provider. You may select MET Norway, wttr.in or WeatherAPI.com in settings. If an alternative provider fails, the app may fall back to Open-Meteo. WeatherAPI.com requests also include the API key you enter and the selected language.

City search sends the text you type and the selected language to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Current Location and translating a saved city name may use Android's location and geocoding services, which can involve Google or your device's configured service provider. Online providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. They process requests under their own policies; we do not control their retention practices.

  • Open-Meteo, including its city geocoding service.
  • MET Norway.
  • wttr.in.
  • WeatherAPI.com.
  • Google Privacy Policy, where Google services are used.

You may revoke location permission in Android settings. To stop sending a previously selected location to weather services, disable weather in the app and widget settings; revoking GPS permission alone does not remove the saved city or stop requests for its coordinates.

3. Optional device calendar integration

If you grant calendar permission and enable synchronization for an event, the app reads relevant device calendar information and writes event titles, descriptions, dates, times and reminders into the Android system calendar. It may read back events previously created by the app. Your device calendar provider, such as Google Calendar, may synchronize these records with its cloud service under your device's account and sync settings. This is separate from the app's local database and is controlled by that provider.

You can decline or revoke calendar permission. You can turn off synchronization for an event or delete an event in the app; the app attempts to remove the linked system-calendar record when permission allows. If permission is unavailable, or a copy remains elsewhere, delete it directly from your calendar provider.

4. Optional audio recordings

If you choose to record a voice reminder for a habit and grant microphone permission, the app records audio into a file in its private device storage. Recordings are played as reminders or when you choose playback. The app does not upload these recordings to the developer or a speech recognition service. You can remove a recording while editing a habit; deleting the habit also removes its associated recording. You can revoke microphone permission in Android settings.

5. Notifications and alarms

Notification and alarm permissions support date notifications, scheduled reminders and habit alarms. Reminders may use sound, vibration or a full-screen reminder when allowed by Android. These functions use data already stored on your device and do not require uploading reminder content.

6. Advertising, analytics and data sales

The current Google Play edition contains no advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK or developer crash-reporting service. The developer does not sell personal data. If features affecting data handling change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated.

7. Storage, security, retention and deletion

App data is held in Android app-private storage. Weather and city-search requests use HTTPS. The app's optional API key is stored as an app preference; the app does not implement an additional application-level encryption layer for its database, recordings or preferences.

Saved content remains on the device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall. Weather cache and selected location may be replaced during updates or when you select a different city. You can delete notes, events and habits using their in-app controls. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → this app → Storage → Clear storage. Uninstalling removes the app's local storage, but does not necessarily remove copies in a system calendar or an existing backup.

The app allows Android backup. Depending on your device, account and backup settings, Android or your device manufacturer may back up or transfer eligible app data, including preferences, database records and recordings. We do not operate that backup service. Manage or delete backups through your device or account provider. Clearing local storage alone does not delete existing backups or copies synchronized by a calendar provider.

If you contact us by email, we receive the address and information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and retain correspondence only as needed for support or applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the contact above. The app does not automatically attach your notes, recordings or location to such emails.

This policy page does not contain advertising, analytics scripts or a data-entry form. Its hosting provider can process ordinary request information, including IP addresses and server logs, under its own privacy policy. For GitHub Pages hosting, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
